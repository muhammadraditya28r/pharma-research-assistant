## 01. Advanced quantification of tris(hydroxymethyl)aminomethane (THAM) in solid adsorbents: comparing UV-Vis, HPLC-UV, and HPLC-fluorescence

**Instrument:** HPLC-FL, HPLC-UV, UV-Vis  
**Category:** Method development  
**Published:** 2026-11-26  
**Journal:** Analytical Methods

**Research finding:** The study compares three analytical methods for quantifying THAM in solid adsorbents. HPLC-FL was found to be the most sensitive (LOD 0.017 wt%), while UV-Vis was the simplest.

**Practical relevance:** Provides a reliable basis for THAM quantification in solid adsorbents like zeolites and porous silica, with potential adaptation for other amino alcohols.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Analytical method selection: Balancing sensitivity and simplicity in solid adsorbent characterization.

**Reference:** [Advanced quantification of tris(hydroxymethyl)aminomethane (THAM) in solid adsorbents: comparing UV-Vis, HPLC-UV, and HPLC-fluorescence](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ay01458e)

## 02. Simultaneous determination of dapagliflozin and bisoprolol by LC–MS/MS: method development, validation, and forced degradation studies

**Instrument:** LC-MS/MS  
**Category:** Validation  
**Published:** 2026-11-25  
**Journal:** Chemical Papers

**Research finding:** A stability-indicating LC-MS/MS method was developed and validated for the simultaneous measurement of bisoprolol and dapagliflozin in tablet dosage forms and bulk substances.

**Practical relevance:** The method successfully separated analytes and demonstrated stability-indicating capability under acidic, basic, oxidative, thermal, and photolytic stress conditions.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Ensuring stability and quality in combination therapies: A robust LC-MS/MS approach.

**Reference:** [Simultaneous determination of dapagliflozin and bisoprolol by LC–MS/MS: method development, validation, and forced degradation studies](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1007/s11696-026-06054-z)

## 03. Green fatty-acid-based hydrophobic NADES with ultrasonic-assisted dispersive liquid–liquid micro extraction for fluorometric detection of rhodamine 6G in cosmetic products

**Instrument:** Fluorescence spectroscopy  
**Category:** Sample preparation  
**Published:** 2026-11-19  
**Journal:** Analytical Methods

**Research finding:** A green UA-DLLME method using hydrophobic natural deep eutectic solvents (HNADES) was developed for the sensitive detection of rhodamine 6G in cosmetics.

**Practical relevance:** Offers a sustainable, solvent-reduced analytical strategy with high enrichment factors and excellent green analytical performance metrics.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Greening the lab: Sustainable sample preparation for cosmetic quality control.

**Reference:** [Green fatty-acid-based hydrophobic NADES with ultrasonic-assisted dispersive liquid–liquid micro extraction for fluorometric detection of rhodamine 6G in cosmetic products](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ay01341d)

## 04. Quantification of antimicrobials and surfactants in drain biofilms: rapid extraction and comprehensive LC-MS/MS analysis

**Instrument:** LC-MS/MS  
**Category:** Sample preparation  
**Published:** 2026-11-19  
**Journal:** Analytical Methods

**Research finding:** Developed a robust sample preparation workflow and LC-MS/MS method for the simultaneous analysis of diverse antimicrobials and surfactants in complex biofilm matrices.

**Practical relevance:** Enables rapid multi-class quantification of residues in biofilms, which are reservoirs for antimicrobial resistance, using internal standard normalization to account for matrix effects.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Tackling antimicrobial resistance: A new analytical workflow for complex biofilm matrices.

**Reference:** [Quantification of antimicrobials and surfactants in drain biofilms: rapid extraction and comprehensive LC-MS/MS analysis](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ay01238h)

## 05. Endogenous Metabolite-Mediated Alterations of Drug Ion Intensity in Electrospray Ionization across LC-ESI-MS and DESI-MSI

**Instrument:** LC-ESI-MS, DESI-MSI  
**Category:** Method reliability  
**Published:** 2026-11-24  
**Journal:** Analytical Chemistry

**Research finding:** Investigated signal modulation of psychoactive drugs in tissues and biofluids, identifying trimethylglycine and stachydrine as endogenous suppressors of drug ion signals.

**Practical relevance:** Demonstrates that ion suppression is a pairwise phenomenon, providing a strategy to identify and validate endogenous modulators of analyte ionization in MS.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Solving the matrix effect puzzle: Understanding endogenous ion suppression in MS.

**Reference:** [Endogenous Metabolite-Mediated Alterations of Drug Ion Intensity in Electrospray Ionization across LC-ESI-MS and DESI-MSI](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.6c04639)

## 06. Dispersive Magnetic Solid-Phase Cleanup Technique for Analysis of Multiclass Herbicides in Fresh Produce Prior to UPLC-MS Detection

**Instrument:** UPLC-QTOF-MS  
**Category:** Sample preparation  
**Published:** 2026-11-25  
**Journal:** Food Analytical Methods

**Research finding:** Evaluated a magnetic nanocomposite (Fe3O4@NH2-GO) as a reusable cleanup sorbent for removing matrix interferants from complex food samples prior to herbicide analysis.

**Practical relevance:** Provides an effective, reusable cleanup method for pigment-rich, lipid-rich, and sugar-rich food matrices, offering a sustainable alternative to traditional QuEChERS.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Advancing food safety: Reusable magnetic sorbents for complex matrix cleanup.

**Reference:** [Dispersive Magnetic Solid-Phase Cleanup Technique for Analysis of Multiclass Herbicides in Fresh Produce Prior to UPLC-MS Detection](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1007/s12161-026-03355-7)

## 07. A DoE-optimized QuEChERS–HRMS method for the simultaneous determination of pesticides and pharmaceuticals in dairy products

**Instrument:** HRMS  
**Category:** Optimization  
**Published:** 2026-11-18  
**Journal:** Analytical Methods

**Research finding:** Developed a DoE-optimized QuEChERS-HRMS method to simultaneously determine ten pesticides and four pharmaceuticals in dairy products using a single acidified acetate/PSA condition.

**Practical relevance:** Consolidates two compound classes usually requiring separate protocols into one workflow, improving efficiency for dairy-safety monitoring.

**LinkedIn discussion angle:** Efficiency in the lab: Consolidating pesticide and pharmaceutical analysis in dairy products.

**Reference:** [A DoE-optimized QuEChERS–HRMS method for the simultaneous determination of pesticides and pharmaceuticals in dairy products](https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ay02226j)
